# 📞 Customer Experience Analysis Using E-Commerce Customer Support Data

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer experience plays a vital role in customer satisfaction, loyalty, and business growth in the e-commerce industry. This project analyzes customer support data to evaluate service performance, measure customer satisfaction, identify operational challenges, and generate actionable business insights.

Using **Microsoft Excel**, the project transforms raw customer support records into meaningful visualizations through data cleaning, KPI calculations, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and an interactive dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

### Primary Objectives

- Analyze customer support interactions and service performance.
- Evaluate factors influencing customer satisfaction.
- Measure key customer support KPIs.
- Develop an interactive Excel dashboard.
- Generate business insights for improving customer retention and service quality.

### Analytical Goals

- Analyze customer satisfaction across support channels.
- Evaluate response and resolution times.
- Compare issue categories based on ticket volume.
- Study customer interactions across communication channels.
- Identify trends affecting customer experience.
- Build an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

---

## 📂 Dataset

The project uses an **E-Commerce Customer Support Dataset** containing customer service interactions, including:

- Customer Support Tickets
- Support Channels
- Issue Categories
- Response Time
- Resolution Time
- Customer Satisfaction Ratings
- Ticket Status
- Communication Details

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Tickets
- Average Satisfaction Score
- Average Response Time
- Average Resolution Time
- Resolution Rate
- Issue Category Distribution
- Support Channel Performance

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Faster response and resolution times improve customer satisfaction.
- Frequently reported issue categories indicate areas requiring operational improvements.
- Customer interactions vary across Email, Chat, and Phone support channels.
- Efficient resolution significantly enhances overall customer experience.
- Dashboard-driven monitoring enables better service management and decision-making.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

- Improve response times to enhance customer satisfaction.
- Prioritize high-frequency issue categories.
- Strengthen support channels with higher customer demand.
- Continuously monitor KPIs using dashboards.
- Utilize customer feedback to improve products and services.
- Implement proactive support strategies to reduce recurring issues.
